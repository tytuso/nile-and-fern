# A quick Nile & Fern walkthrough

1. Start on the public homepage. Open Destinations to reveal six real Uganda places. Use the journey finder to choose a style and length.
2. Save two journeys with their heart buttons. Open Saved journeys to compare their length, destinations and pace. Reload to demonstrate the device-local shortlist.
3. Open a journey, expand its day-by-day outline and download a real sample PDF.
4. Choose Make this journey mine. Review the preselected destination interests, trip length and stay preferences. Enter fictional sample contact details, review and submit.
5. Save the receipt and private code. Track the enquiry with both values. The result gives limited status information, not the original contact data.
6. Open Operator studio, then the practice workspace. Review a fictional enquiry, change its status, edit the public summary, add a private team note and export a CSV. Practice changes stay on the current device and can be reset.
7. The connected workspace uses assigned Nile Core staff access. It reviews actual sample enquiries from the forms. A staff status update becomes visible through the visitor’s reference/code tracking page; private notes remain in the studio.
8. Explore Field notes, the FAQ search, photo credits and the install-app guidance. Ask Fern is a dormant assistant preview.

The company and sample journeys are fictional. No payment, real booking, permit reservation or automatic email is triggered. Demonstrate with fictional traveller details.
